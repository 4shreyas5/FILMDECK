ArcDesk is a fictional help-desk software company. This brief is fictional and was written only to test FilmDeck.

Monthly customer churn rose from 3% to 7% over the last two quarters.

Exit surveys show 41% of leaving customers named price as the main reason, compared with 12% who named missing features.

Competitors are offering discounts of 15–25% to ArcDesk's mid-size accounts. ArcDesk's own loyalty discount is 5–8%.

Support response time is unchanged at 2 hours, and satisfaction scores have stayed flat.

The leadership team wants a plan that protects mid-size accounts without starting a price war.
