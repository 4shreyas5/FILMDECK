title: The library that came back
fictional: true
---
This is a fictional story written only to test FilmDeck. It contains no figures on purpose.

The village library was nearly closed. Few people came, the roof leaked and the shelves were out of date.

A group of neighbours began to meet there on weekday evenings to share stories and repair old books.

Soon children joined them. A retired teacher started a reading circle, and the local baker donated tea and cake.

The council noticed the change. It agreed to fix the roof and keep the library open.

The lesson the neighbours took away is that people look after places that they feel belong to them.
