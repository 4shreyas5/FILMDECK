Tidewell is a fictional start-up that sells water-leak sensors. This strategy note is fictional and exists only to test FilmDeck.

Three customer groups were considered: home owners, property managers and municipal water utilities.

Property managers would pay $15–$20 per sensor per month. Home owners resisted any monthly fee above $5.

Municipal utilities buy slowly but sign contracts of 3 years or more.

The first-year goal is 200 customers, all in one segment, rather than a little of everything.

The founders recommend focusing on property managers first and treating utilities as a later step.
