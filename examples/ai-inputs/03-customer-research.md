Northlight Bank is a fictional bank. This research summary is fictional and written only to test FilmDeck.

We interviewed 20 customers who had stopped using the mobile app within the last year.

14 of the 20 said the login screen was confusing. 6 said unexpected fees were the reason they stopped.

One customer put it this way: "I never know where to start."

Customers who stayed described the app as "fast but hard to find things in".

The research team believes the biggest gap is first-time guidance, not features.
