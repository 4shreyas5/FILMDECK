Brightleaf Tea is a fictional company. These campaign results are fictional and exist only to test FilmDeck.

The spring campaign tested a new short-video creative against the old banner ads. Click-through rate rose from 1.8% to 3.4%.

Cost per sign-up fell from $12 to $7. The total budget stayed at $40,000.

The email list grew by 5,200 subscribers during the campaign.

Short video was the best-performing channel. Banner ads were the weakest.

The team recommends moving money from banners to short video and keeping the weekly email.
