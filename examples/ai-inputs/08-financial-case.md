Solaris Cafe is a fictional cafe chain. This business case is fictional and written only to test FilmDeck.

Replacing the old ovens needs an investment of ₹18 lakh.

The new ovens are expected to save ₹7.5 lakh a year in energy and repairs, which gives a payback of 2.4 years.

The bank loan for the purchase carries an interest rate of 9.5%.

The old ovens already break down about once a month, and each breakdown stops baking for a day.

Finance recommends approving the investment in the next budget round.
