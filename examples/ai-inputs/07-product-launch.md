Lumo is a fictional sleep-tracking app. This launch plan is fictional and written only to test FilmDeck.

The private beta runs from January 15 to February 20 with 500 testers.

Public launch is planned for March 4. Support ticket #4471 raised a sync bug that must be fixed first.

The launch will include 4 features: sleep score, smart alarm, bedtime reminders and weekly report.

Early testers rated the smart alarm the most useful feature and the weekly report the least.

The launch team wants a calm, confident rollout and a clear message about why Lumo is different.
