Kestrel Freight is a fictional company. This HR review is fictional and written only to test FilmDeck.

22% of new hires leave within their first 90 days. The company average is 9%.

New hires meet their manager for a first one-to-one on day 11, on average.

Engagement scores for people in their first year are 61, against 78 for longer-serving staff.

Exit interviews mention unclear roles and no early feedback.

The people team proposes a structured first month with a named buddy and a week-one check-in.
