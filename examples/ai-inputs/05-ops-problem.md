fictional: true

Harborline is a fictional logistics company. This operations review is fictional and written only to test FilmDeck.

The average order cycle time is 52 hours. The target is 24 hours.

38% of delays happen at the packing stage, more than at picking or shipping.

During the peak season, order volume rises to 3 times the normal level, but packing capacity does not change.

Night shifts are understaffed at packing while day shifts are overloaded.

The recommended fix is more packing stations and rebalancing shifts, not a new warehouse system.
